# Starbie---Half-Life-Week-1

So made this as my first ever PCB that will actually work (fingers crossed) as part of week 1 of Hack Club Half Life.

***So what is Starbie?***

Starbie is a tiny motion controlled digital pet, in the shape of a star, with 2 tiny gameplay buttons. Instead of pressing much, you interact with board physcially. 
It is meant to sit on your desk, show you persoanlity, be with you on the go, or whatever you want for it to be.

***What is it made of***

Starbie is made of a microcontorller, oled display, motion sensor, environment senor, and 2 buttons. It is USB powered and is beginner friendly (for me :).. )
